# Recruitment Webapp
The Recruitment Webapp is a streamlined, dual-sided platform designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and hiring teams. By combining a frictionless, conversion-optimized candidate registration interface with an intuitive, visual admin dashboard, the platform eliminates the administrative overhead typical of early-stage recruitment pipelines.

## Strategic Objectives
→ Time-to-Hire Reduction: Accelerate candidate screening by introducing immediate visual indicators for core qualifications.  
→ Frictionless Sourcing: Maximize candidate completion rates through a clean, single-page application form.  
→ Instant Credibility: Build a trustworthy, high-performance experience that demonstrates system reliability and workflow speed to HR professionals.  

## Product Scope
The platform is strictly bounded by two functional areas to maintain a lightweight, high-speed footprint

Public Candidate Registration Form  
A clean, trustworthy landing page allowing applicants to submit their profiles directly.  
→ Data Capture: Full Name, Email, Phone Number, Age, Country, City, and English Level.  
→ Document Attachment: A mandatory PDF CV upload.  

Admin Management Panel  
A centralized dashboard enabling HR teams to view, filter, and process applications.  
- The Candidate Table: A master view displaying all applicants with robust filtering capabilities.
- Traffic-Light Language System: An automated visual marker for English proficiency:
   - Green: Advanced proficiencies ($B2+$)
   - Yellow: Intermediate proficiency ($B1$)
   - Red: Beginner/Lower proficiencies ($A1, A2$)

- Profile View & State Management: Ability to drill down into a candidate’s specific details and explicitly transition their application state to "Accepted", "Rejected", or "In Review".

## Results
→ Efficiency: Achieve a low drop-off rate on the public registration form due to its straightforward, single-page layout.  
→ Screening Velocity: HR users should be able to evaluate and categorize a candidate's language fit within 2 seconds of viewing the admin table, thanks to the automated traffic-light system.  
→ Data Integrity: 100% enforcement of the mandatory PDF format constraint at the API boundary, protecting the database from corrupted or unsupported file attachments.
